Freitex Clearing & forwarding OS

An operating system for clearing and forwarding agents across Africa. Quotes become jobs, milestones and settlements, and import duty is computed from versioned tax data.

01Overview

Agents price an itemised quote, open it as a job, work the milestones, settle disbursements against receipts and close. Importers follow along through secure links without an account.

Kenya launches first, but no country is hard-coded: tax rules are versioned data, and Kenya’s vehicle duties match the earlier system to the shilling. An investor demo shows the wider vision on synthetic data.

Screens are from the investor demo, which uses synthetic data.

03Problems solved

Solution

Problem

Solution

Payments designed to settle at the provider, never through the platform.

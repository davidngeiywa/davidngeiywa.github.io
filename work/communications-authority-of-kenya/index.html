Communications Authority of Kenya Website rebuild

01Overview

The Authority’s design system lives in Figma. A generator turns its tokens into both the theme’s styles and the editor’s settings, so the two never drift apart.

Every page is editable without code through a library of 21 reusable widgets. News, tenders with their documents, contracts, publications and downloads were migrated from the legacy site.

Pre-launch. Screens are from the build and its automated QA runs, October 2026.

RCL Africa Logistics operations portal

The operations platform behind a Kenyan vehicle and container clearing business: a duty engine that turns official vehicle values into branded quotes, orders and tracked clearances.

01Overview

A duty engine computes import duty, excise, VAT and registration charges, and the same engine powers the calculator on the client’s website. Quotation and order pipelines cover ordinary imports, special transfers and containers, with stages, documents, payments and WhatsApp updates.

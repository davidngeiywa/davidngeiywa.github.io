01Overview

Thrive HRMS is the HR system of record for its client companies: employee records, payroll, attendance, leave, performance and field operations. I lead the backend and the payroll engine within a team of about twenty engineers.

In 2026 we turned a one-company system into a SaaS with a subdomain per company, keeping existing clients and installed apps working throughout.

Client systems are private, so this page shows the platform shape rather than screens.

02What we built

01

Statutory payroll

PAYE, NSSF, SHIF, Housing Levy, NITA and P9s from configurable, tested formulas.

02

Three ways to clock in

Selfie and location check-ins on mobile, face-match kiosks and biometric devices.

03

Multi-tenant SaaS

A subdomain per company with its own data and onboarding.

04

Performance management

Balanced scorecards that cascade from organisation to individual.

05

Leave & documents

Multi-level approvals, accruals and document expiry alerts.

06

Field force

Visits, orders against targets and market reports for sales teams.

03Problems solved

Problem

Moving a live single-company system to multi-tenancy.

Solution

A staged migration with tenant-aware data and storage, plus backfill tooling for existing clients.

Problem

Payroll has to be right every month.

Solution

Money precision rules, duplicate-run prevention and unit tests on the statutory calculations.

Problem

Matching faces reliably at a busy entrance.

Solution

On-device face detection before a cloud face match, behind a swappable provider interface.
